How we work

A clear process, from idea to launch

Five steps, each with a clear deliverable, so you always know where your project stands.

  1. Discover

    We learn who your learners are and what they should be able to do. You get a short written plan.

  2. Design

    We sketch the screens and learning flow, then share a clickable prototype for your feedback.

  3. Build

    We develop in short cycles, and you see a working version every couple of weeks.

  4. Test and launch

    We test on real devices, prepare the store listing and publish the app.

  5. Support and improve

    We fix issues, ship updates and add content based on how learners use the app.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply within one business day.

What is the fastest way to reach you?

Email us at enhancerworx@gmail.com, or use the form in the contact section. We reply within one business day.

Where can I get your apps?

All our apps are on Google Play. Use the Install button next to any app above, or open our Google Play page.

Who are your apps for?

Our learning apps are designed for learners aged 13 and over, including school students, exam aspirants and developers. Our casual games Snake Word, Merge Balls 2048 and Rock Balance Master are suitable for all ages.

Are your apps free? Do they show ads?

You can install them from Google Play. Each Google Play listing states whether an app contains ads or in-app purchases, so check it before you install.

Do the apps work offline?

Many do, including our map, history and biology apps. The app’s description on Google Play says whether it works offline.

Are your apps available on iPhone?

Our own apps are currently on Google Play for Android. We do build iOS apps for client projects.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Learning apps, exam-prep apps, educational games, programming-course apps, web learning portals and white-label platforms for schools, institutes, publishers and training companies.

How long will my project take and what will it cost?

Both depend on scope. Tell us what you need through the contact form. After a short call we send a written estimate for time and cost.

Will you publish the app for me?

Yes. We can prepare the store listing, build the release and publish it, and keep the app updated afterwards.

Who owns the code and content of a custom app?

Ownership and licensing are agreed in writing before work starts, so there are no surprises.

I found a bug or want a feature in one of your apps.

Email enhancerworx@gmail.com with the app name, your phone model and what happened. We read every message.

Where are each app’s privacy policy and terms of service?

Every app has its own privacy policy and terms of service. You can also open any app above and select the links in its details. Our website privacy policy is here.

Tell us what you have in mind. We reply within one business day with clear next steps.
