How a project works

Five steps, with a clear deliverable at the end of each one.

  1. Discover

    We learn who your learners are and what they should be able to do. You get a short written plan.

  2. Design

    We sketch the screens and learning flow, then show you a clickable prototype to react to.

  3. Build

    We develop the app in short cycles, and you see a working version every couple of weeks.

  4. Test and launch

    We test on real devices, prepare the store listing and publish the app.

  5. Support and improve

    We fix issues, ship updates and add content based on how learners use the app.

Small studio. One focus: learning.

Enhancerworx started in 2018 with a simple belief: learning apps should be easy to start, pleasant to use and rewarding to finish. We build them for Android, iOS and the web, and we keep every one of them up to date after launch.

  • Designed around the learner

    Every screen is built for how people study, with short sessions, clear feedback and visible progress.

  • Works without internet

    Many of our apps work offline, so learning is not tied to a connection.

  • Practice that motivates

    Quizzes, badges, streaks and progress tracking give learners a reason to come back.

  • One team, every platform

    Android, iOS and web products share one design language and one point of contact.
