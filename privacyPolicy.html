Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Introduction

At Enhancerworx, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information that you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your personal data when you visit our website or use our services.

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you give us voluntarily, such as when you contact us or register for our services. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if you choose to give it
  • Billing information, if applicable

How we use your information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To respond to your enquiries
  • To send updates about our services, where you have asked for them

Our website and the contact form

When you send a message through the contact form on this website, your name, email address and message are passed to Formspree, a form-handling service, which emails them to our inbox. If you fill in the optional fields, your phone number, organisation, project details, budget and timeline are sent in the same way. We use this information only to reply to you.

Formspree processes this data on our behalf and has its own privacy policy. This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply to you.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Our apps

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked on its Google Play listing under App support, and its own terms of service.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set tracking cookies. It stores one small preference in your browser, your choice of light or dark theme, so that the site looks the way you left it. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Data security

This website is served over HTTPS. We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration or destruction.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. You may also withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email us at enhancerworx@gmail.com.
