1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Enhancerworx (“Enhancerworx”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the mobile application Merge Balls 2048: Drop Puzzle (package name com.enhancerworx.mergeballs2048) (the “App”), available on Google Play.

We built the App to help people learn, not to collect data. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. Most of what you do in the App stays on your device. Some information is collected by trusted third-party services that help us show ads, understand how the App is used, fix problems and send notifications. This policy explains what that information is, why it is collected and the choices you have.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. This policy should be read together with the App’s Terms of Service.

3.Information we collect

Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following on your device so it can work and remember your progress. We do not upload it to our servers:

  • Learning progress, scores and quiz results
  • Settings and preferences

This information is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. If your device’s own Android backup is turned on, Android may back up App data to your Google account under Google’s terms.

Information collected automatically by third-party services

When you use the App, the services described in the next section may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, an app-instance ID and a push-notification token;
  • Device and network information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, mobile carrier and IP address;
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address (the App does not access your precise GPS location);
  • Usage information, such as screens viewed, features used, session length, and when you install, open or update the App;
  • Ad information, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • Diagnostics and log data, such as crash reports, error logs, the App’s configuration at the time of an error, and the date and time of use.

Information you choose to send us

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the App.

We do not ask for, and the App does not collect, your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment card details. Any purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

5.How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide the App’s features and remember your progress on your device;
  • show ads, which keep the App free to download, and measure how they perform;
  • understand which lessons and features are useful, so we can improve them;
  • find and fix crashes and other technical problems;
  • send push notifications you have allowed, such as study reminders or news about new content;
  • reply to your messages and support requests;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security problems, including invalid ad activity;
  • comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We process this information on the basis of your consent (for example, when you allow notifications or agree to personalised ads), to provide the App you asked for, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the App working, secure and free.

6.Device permissions

The App asks only for the permissions it needs:

Internet and network accessTo load ads, send analytics and crash reports, and download content where needed.
NotificationsTo show push notifications. On Android 13 and later the App asks your permission first.
Advertising IDUsed by Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.

You can review or withdraw permissions at any time in your phone’s Settings > Apps > Merge Balls 2048: Drop Puzzle > Permissions.

7.Advertising and your ad choices

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your activity in this and other apps, or non-personalised, depending on your choices and the law where you live.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice from the App’s settings or privacy options where available.

You can also control ads on your device:

  • to delete or reset your advertising ID, open Settings > Privacy > Ads on your Android device (the wording may differ slightly on some phones);
  • to manage ad personalisation across Google services, visit My Ad Center.

Because the App is suitable for all ages, it may be used by children. Where the App knows or is told that a user is a child, ads are limited to non-personalised ads suitable for children, in line with Google Play’s Families policy.

8.Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may send you reminders and news about new content through OneSignal. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s Settings > Notifications, or in the App’s settings where available.

9.Cookies and similar technologies

The App does not use cookies itself. However, the third-party services listed above may use cookies, software development kits (SDKs) and similar technologies, such as device identifiers, to collect information and improve their services. You can limit this using the ad and notification choices described in this policy. If you refuse some of these technologies, parts of the App may not work as intended.

10.How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed above, who process it to provide their services to us and are not allowed to use it for other purposes except as described in their own policies;
  • when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a public authority;
  • when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Enhancerworx, our users or others, including to prevent fraud;
  • if Enhancerworx is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case the information would remain protected by this policy or an equivalent one.

11.Where information is processed

Enhancerworx is based in India. Our service providers may store and process information in other countries, including the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, these providers use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect your information.

12.How long information is kept

Information stored on your device stays there until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Information collected by third-party services is kept for the period set by each provider and by our settings with them, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and keep a record of our conversation.

13.Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable measures to protect information, and we choose service providers that protect data with industry-standard security. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14.Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • withdraw consent you have given, such as for personalised ads or notifications;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • complain to a data-protection authority.

Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot link information held by third-party services to you by name. To make a request, email us at enhancerworx@gmail.com with the App’s name and details of your request. We will respond within the time required by law. You can also stop all collection by the App at any time by uninstalling it.

15.Children’s privacy

The App is a casual game suitable for all ages, so children may use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is permitted by law and by Google Play’s Families policy, and we do not ask children to provide personal information. Where a user is a child, ads are limited to non-personalised ads suitable for children.

Parents and guardians can manage their child’s use of the App with Google Play parental controls and Google Family Link. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

16.Links to other sites

The App may contain links to websites, videos or other apps that we do not operate. If you follow such a link, the other party’s privacy policy applies. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, their content or practices.

17.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change service providers. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to tell you, for example through the App. Please review this page periodically.

18.Contact us

If you have any questions, requests or complaints about this Privacy Policy or how the App handles information, please contact us. We aim to reply within a few working days.
